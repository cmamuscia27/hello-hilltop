Neighbor notes

Little notes that aren't quite events: a park reminder, a found dog, a thank-you. Hello! Hilltop writes these. The automatic news strip never erases them. When a note has an end date, it steps aside on its own.

No neighbor notes right now. When there's something worth sharing, it'll land here and stay until it expires.

Headlines from local news live on the home page. Have something for the calendar? Host an event.

Hello! Hilltop is a neighbor-run community group in Hilltop, Denver, separate from the Hilltop Neighborhood Association. We're not a 501(c)(3), so gifts aren't tax-deductible. Made with 🧡 by neighbors.
