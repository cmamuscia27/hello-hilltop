Who we are

Hello, neighbor!

Hello! Hilltop is a brand-new, neighbor-run group made up of people who missed knowing each other. We missed the block parties, the wave from the porch, and the "come on over, the kids are playing in the yard." So we decided to bring it back.

We're starting from scratch. No big budget, no fancy office. Just neighbors who believe Hilltop is better when we show up for each other. We'll share our intentions, ask for yours, and be open about every dollar and every plan. Anyone and everyone of any age is welcome. 'Cause that's a community, after all.

Our Neighbor Promises

  1. Everyone's welcome. Renters, owners, kids, grandparents, newcomers, old-timers.
  2. Fun first. If it isn't bringing people together, we rethink it.
  3. Open books. We share costs, plans, and decisions out loud.
  4. Your ideas matter. We share our intentions and ask for yours.
