Your turn

Host an event

A porch hang, a park meetup, a pie walk. Tell us about it and we'll read it before it goes on the calendar. Your email is how we ask a question. It is never shown publicly.

Be a good host

  • Keep it neighborly. A porch, a park, a living room, a block.
  • Follow Denver's rules. Parks, noise, and food all have them.
  • Don't close a street or a sidewalk unless the city has given you the permit.
  • We'll read this before it goes on the calendar. We can't issue a permit, and we may say not yet.
Good to know (optional)

Hello! Hilltop is a neighbor-run community group in Hilltop, Denver, separate from the Hilltop Neighborhood Association. We're not a 501(c)(3), so gifts aren't tax-deductible. Made with 🧡 by neighbors.
