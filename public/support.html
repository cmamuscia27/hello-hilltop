Support the block.

Hello! Hilltop runs on neighbors. A year of membership, or a one-time gift of any amount, pays for getting people in the same park: the events, and the notes that tell everyone about them.

Not tax-deductible

We're a grassroots neighbors-for-neighbors group. Hello! Hilltop is not a registered nonprofit and is not a 501(c)(3). Contributions are not tax-deductible. You'll get a thank-you, not a tax receipt.

Where the money goes

Events and communications. That means park permits, flyers, the newsletter, and the small costs of getting the word out. It does not mean a salary. We'll keep saying what things cost, out loud. Open books was one of the neighbor promises, and it still is.
